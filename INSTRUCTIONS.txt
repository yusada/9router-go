How to use this package:
1. Extract the ZIP file into the root directory of your 9router-go repository.
2. The Dockerfile and railway.json will be placed in the root directory.
3. Open your README.md and copy the contents of `README_ADDITION.md` into it.
4. Commit and push these changes to GitHub.
5. Go to Railway.com, link your repository, and it will be recognized as a template!
